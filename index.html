🌳 About Cedar Valley Zoo

Cedar Valley Zoo is a zoo project inspired by Planet Zoo. Our mission is to care for animals, celebrate wildlife and help people appreciate the incredible creatures that share our planet.

🐾 Meet Our Animals

Toco Toucan

A colourful bird famous for its enormous orange beak!

Javan Green Magpie

A striking green forest bird with a very special appearance.

Long-tailed Parakeet

A lively parakeet with a beautiful, elongated tail.

Great Hornbill

A magnificent bird with a large, distinctive casque above its beak.

Secretary Bird

A remarkable African bird known for its long legs and dramatic head feathers.

Wild Boar

A powerful wild pig with tusks and a strong sense of smell.

Komodo Dragon

The world's largest living lizard, native to Indonesian islands.

Hawksbill Sea Turtle

A marine turtle with a distinctive beak and beautifully patterned shell.

Moon Jelly

A delicate jellyfish that drifts through the water with gentle movements.

Clown Anemonefish

A bright reef fish famous for its orange body and white stripes.

Blue Tang

A colourful blue reef fish that stands out among coral reefs.

Red Lionfish

A striking fish with fan-like fins and venomous spines.

🪲 Sacred Scarab Beetle

A fascinating beetle inspired by ancient Egyptian symbolism. Sacred scarab beetles are associated with dung rolling and recycling nutrients in their environment.
