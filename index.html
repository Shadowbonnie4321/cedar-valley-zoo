🌱 About Our Zoo

Cedar Valley Zoo is a place to discover wildlife, appreciate nature, and celebrate the animals that make our world special.

Our Mission

We believe that every animal deserves respect, care, and a suitable environment. Our zoo celebrates biodiversity and inspires visitors to appreciate the natural world.

From colourful birds to fascinating reptiles and amazing aquatic animals, there is always something new to discover at Cedar Valley Zoo.

🐾 Meet Our Animals

Welcome to our animal directory! Explore our birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians, invertebrates, and aquatic animals. All our animals are together in this one section.

Toco Toucan

A spectacular tropical bird recognised for its enormous, colourful bill.

Javan Green Magpie

A striking green forest bird native to Java in Indonesia.

Long-tailed Parakeet

A colourful parakeet with a distinctive long tail.

Great Hornbill

A magnificent forest bird with a large, impressive bill and casque.

Secretary Bird

A remarkable African bird of prey with long legs, a distinctive crest, and a famous snake-stomping hunting technique.

Wild Boar

A powerful wild pig with a sturdy body, a strong snout, and impressive tusks.

Hawksbill Sea Turtle

A critically endangered marine turtle known for its beautiful patterned shell and pointed beak.

Komodo Dragon

The world's largest living lizard, native to several Indonesian islands.

Moon Jelly

A delicate jellyfish with a translucent bell that drifts through the water.

Clown Anemonefish

A bright orange reef fish with white bands that lives among sea anemones.

Blue Tang

A vivid blue tropical reef fish with a distinctive shape and yellow tail.

Red Lionfish

A striking marine fish with red-and-white stripes and long venomous spines.
