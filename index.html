Welcome to Cedar Valley Zoo

A Planet Zoo 2 creation by Harvey

Cedar Valley Zoo is a growing wildlife park created in Planet Zoo 2. Our goal is to create beautiful habitats, celebrate the incredible diversity of animals and build a place where every creature matters.

From colourful birds soaring through our aviary to the amazing animals we hope to welcome in the future, every new addition helps our zoo grow.

Our promise: Every animal has a story, every habitat is an adventure, and every goodbye deserves to be remembered.
