🐾 Meet Our Animals

Get to know the amazing residents of Cedar Valley Zoo. Our collection will grow as we build new habitats!

Toco Toucan

The Toco Toucan is famous for its enormous orange beak. Despite its size, the beak is surprisingly lightweight. This bird lives in South America and spends much of its time among the trees.

Its long beak helps it reach fruit, and it plays an important role in spreading seeds through tropical environments.

Habitat: Tropical forest

Diet: Mainly fruit

Javan Green Magpie

Native to the forests of Java, Indonesia, the Javan Green Magpie is known for its vivid green plumage and striking dark facial markings.

These intelligent members of the crow family benefit from complex habitats where they can explore, perch and search for food.

Habitat: Tropical forest

Diet: Invertebrates and small animals

Our Aviary

Long-tailed Parakeet

The Long-tailed Parakeet is a colourful parrot from Southeast Asia. Its long tail gives it an elegant shape as it moves between branches.

Active and curious, these birds benefit from space to climb, perch and explore their surroundings.

Habitat: Forest and woodland

Diet: Fruit, seeds and plant matter

Our Aviary

Great Hornbill

The Great Hornbill is a magnificent forest bird with a huge curved bill and a distinctive casque on top. It lives in parts of South and Southeast Asia.

Great Hornbills help forests regenerate by spreading seeds after eating fruit. They are an important part of their forest ecosystems.

Habitat: Tropical forest

Diet: Mainly fruit, plus small animals
